About Us

Where Character Meets Knowledge

Radiant Priesthood Academy is a learning community where the motto Magnitudo in servitium — “greatness in service” — inspires learners to grow in knowledge, character, and care for others.

We aim to nurture confident thinkers and responsible leaders through academic learning, creativity, teamwork, and service.

  • ✔ A values-led approach to learning
  • ✔ Support for academic and personal growth
  • ✔ Opportunities for creativity and leadership
  • ✔ Respect, responsibility, and community service
